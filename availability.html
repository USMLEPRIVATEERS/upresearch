1 · How do you want to participate?

Everyone presents eventually — and there's a path to get there. Start by reading a question, then check the methods of someone else's article, then present your own: Question reader→Methods checker→Presenter. Each step is a bit more preparation than the last, so the first presentation never has to feel like a leap.

📄 Your article

Presenters must attach the article they will discuss. Prefer a link to the full manuscript (journal website, PubMed Central, DOI). If the full text is not freely available online, upload the PDF to your Google Drive, set sharing to “Anyone with the link”, and paste the Drive link here.
